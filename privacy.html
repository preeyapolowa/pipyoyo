Privacy Policy — Pipyoyo Uploader

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Pipyoyo Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pipyoyo YouTube channel (@pipyoyokids) and the Pipyoyo Facebook Page to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public or to other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publishing times.
  • The owner's own Facebook Page, through the Facebook Graph API, to publish and schedule the owner's own posts and reels.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used only to perform the uploads described above.
  • The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or any other people.
  • The tool does not use analytics, cookies, advertising or tracking.

Children's privacy

The Pipyoyo channel publishes content made for children. This tool does not collect any information from children or any other viewers. Videos are marked as "made for kids" on YouTube in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.
