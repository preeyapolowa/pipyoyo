Privacy Policy — paepnueng uploader

Last updated: 3 November 2026

paepnueng uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @paepnueng (ดูแพ๊บนึง). It uploads the owner's own videos from the owner's computer to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What we access

With the owner's permission through Login with TikTok, the tool uses the scopes user.info.basic (to confirm which account is signed in) and video.upload (to send a draft video). It does not read followers, messages, or other users' data.

What we store

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared. No data about other TikTok users is collected, stored, or sold.

Contact

Questions: contact the account owner through the TikTok account @paepnueng.
